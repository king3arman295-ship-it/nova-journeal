Nova Journal started as a shared notebook between three friends who kept sending each other links. It became a publication that explains emerging technology in plain language.

How we work

Every article is reported, edited by a second person, and checked against primary sources. We say what we do not know, and we update stories when the facts change.

We never publish sponsored posts disguised as articles. If we use a product, we say who paid for it.
